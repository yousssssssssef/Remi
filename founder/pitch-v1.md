Remi, a kitchen app in Arabic and English for iPhone and Android.
Snap your grocery receipt and Remi puts the food into your digital kitchen, shows what expires first, and suggests recipes (400, with photos) from what you already have.
Plan the week's meals, share one shopping list with your household, cook step by step with timers, and see calories and protein per meal.
Free: kitchen, list and recipes. Remi Plus (receipt scanning, meal planner, nutrition, Ask Remi AI assistant, whole household): $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year (local prices in AED, SAR, EGP).
Optional: the Remi countertop device, a small screen with a knob and speaker that shows what to use first and guides cooking, $79 one-time, works with Remi Plus.
