Remi, a kitchen app in Arabic and English for iPhone and Android.
Snap your supermarket receipt, in Arabic or English, and Remi adds the food to your kitchen and reminds you before anything goes off.
More than half of its 400 recipes are Arab home cooking (190 Egyptian, plus Lebanese, Moroccan and Gulf dishes), and it suggests tonight's dinner from what you already have.
One shared kitchen and shopping list for the household: invite your spouse or your helper, so nobody buys the same thing twice.
Free: kitchen, expiry reminders, shared list, recipes and 5 receipt scans a month. Remi Plus (unlimited scans, week planner, nutrition, Ask Remi assistant): $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year, with the first 30 days free and no card needed. The first 1,000 households keep $19.99 a year for as long as they stay.
Your receipts and data are never sold, and you can delete everything from Settings in one tap.
