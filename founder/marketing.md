# Marketing: Remi

**Start with Egyptian households (in Egypt and in the Gulf), on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook groups, with one message: "Snap the receipt. Remi reminds you before anything goes off."** No paid ads until the pilot shows week-4 retention. Paid acquisition can afford at most about **$1.30 per install**, so creators and communities have to carry the launch.

## 1. Evidence used

- **Panel:** 1 of 20 said yes on the v2 offer, and that buyer was a Gulf family on a WhatsApp list who wanted the free shared list. 12 passed on effort, 4 on trust and 2 on price. Arab recipes removed the "Western recipes" objection, and nobody asked about the device.
- **Competitors:** no Arabic app combines a pantry, expiry and receipt logging. Recipes and meal plans are crowded and free in Arabic, so don't sell recipes. The biggest complaints about competitors are lost lists and broken sync, then logging that is wrong or slow.
- **Price:** $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year; founding price $19.99 a year.
- **Catalogue:** 190 Egyptian recipes and only 4 Gulf ones, so Egyptian households come first.

## 2. Positioning

1. **(Recommended)** For **Arabic-speaking families who throw out food they forgot they had**, Remi is **the shared kitchen app that reads your supermarket receipt and reminds you before anything goes off**, unlike WhatsApp lists and recipe apps, which only remember what you need to buy.
   - Evidence: "use first" and expiry were the one thing even the "no" buyers would keep using on the free tier (P014, P019). A shared list answers the duplicate-buying habit (P002, P017).
2. For **Egyptian mums running a household**, Remi is **the Arabic kitchen app that knows Egyptian food and Egyptian receipts**, unlike global pantry apps with no Arabic.
   - Evidence: only the recipe objection moved between panels. Use this as the Egypt-specific version.
3. For **households with a helper**, Remi is **one kitchen the whole house, helper included, can keep up to date**, unlike chasing everyone on WhatsApp.
   - Evidence: 9 of 20 said "my helper/mother runs the kitchen". Use this only once helper mode is built.

## 5. Ten hooks (each answers an objection)

1. "I threw out a full bag of coriander. Again." → the "use first" list *(effort/need)*
2. "Can it read an Arabic Carrefour receipt? Watch." *(Arabic receipts don't work)*
3. "We both bought eggs. Again." → shared list *(WhatsApp works fine)*
4. "Dinner from what's already in your fridge, Egyptian style." *(Western recipes)*
5. "Your helper can update the kitchen. You don't have to." *(my helper runs the kitchen)*
6. "Your receipts are never sold. Delete everything in one tap." *(trust)*
7. "Ramadan shopping without the duplicates." *(timing, seasonal)*
8. "Ordered on Talabat? Paste the order. Done." *(no paper receipt; only once built)*
9. "Free is enough for most families." *(another subscription)*
10. "One scan a week. That's the whole habit." *(I won't keep logging)*

Panel quotes are research. Never use them in ads as customer quotes. Disclose every paid or gifted creator post.

## 6. Budget and numbers

- **Budget for the first 30 days: about $300.** Split: creators $200 (gifting plus 1–2 paid posts), Play listing experiments $0, a small boost of the best organic reel $100. No other paid ads.
- **The most you can pay for a customer.** Contribution is $2.19 a month. If a paying household stays 6 months (an estimate: measure it), it's worth about **$13**. At a 10% trial-to-paid rate, that caps spend at **about $1.30 per install**, which is lower than typical paid-social install costs. That's why the channels are organic.
- **Watch weekly:**
  1. Installs → households that scan at least once in week 1. Trigger: below 50%.
  2. Week-4 logging retention. Trigger: below 25% means pause all promotion.
  3. Cost per paying household from creators. Trigger: above $13.
