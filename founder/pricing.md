# Pricing: Remi Plus

**The price: $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year**, with lower regional prices in Egypt and North Africa. Free stays generous. Don't go lower to win buyers: price isn't why they say no (only 2 of 20 cited it), and at $1.99 the CFO's year 1 turns negative.

## 1. What the buyers said (simulated, 19 complete answers on the v2 offer)

Run on `panel/answers`. P020 is left out because they answered in yearly dollars while everyone else answered monthly.

| point | price a month |
| --- | ---: |
| Too cheap to trust (PMC) | $1.00 |
| Least resistance (OPP) | $1.00 |
| Indifference (IPP) | $2.00 |
| Too expensive (PME) | $3.99 |

Acceptable range: **$1.00 to $3.99**. On the v1 offer it was $1.01 to $4.98. The ranges are wide and low because most buyers weren't going to pay at any price, so their answers are anchored on "free". Use them to choose what to test, not to set the price.

## 2. What competitors charge (`competitors.md`, with links)

- **Kitchen apps, monthly:** $2.99 (Mealime) to $6.99 (Samsung Food+, AED 24.99). The median is about **$4.97**.
- **Kitchen apps, yearly:** $6.99 (NoWaste) to $59.99 (Samsung Food+). The median is about **$36**.
- **Lifetime deals:** $29.99 to $44.99 (KitchenPal, NoWaste).
- **The same job, paid locally:** Gulf households already pay talabat pro (AED 39 a month) or Careem Plus (AED 29 a month) for food convenience.

At $2.99 Remi is the cheapest monthly kitchen app with a pantry, and the only one in Arabic. At $24.99 a year it's below the yearly median.

## 3. What the business needs (CFO tool, founders unpaid)

| price a month | contribution | break-even | year 1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $3.99 | $3.04 | 36 paying households | +$1,589 |
| **$2.99** | **$2.04** | **54** | **+$639** |
| $2.08 (= $24.99 a year) | $1.13 | 96 | -$225 |
| $1.67 (= $19.99 founding) | $0.72 | 151 | -$615 |
| $1.99 | — | 105 | -$311 |

The tool books the store fee as a flat $0.60, so it understates contribution on the cheaper yearly plans (the real fee is 15%). At $24.99 a year the real contribution is about $1.42 a month. Yearly plans earn less per month, but they're paid up front, so they bring cash in early and cut churn. Keep yearly as the default, but watch the mix.

## 4. The ladder

| tier | price | what's in it | why step up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | Kitchen, expiry reminders, shared household list (spouse and helper), all recipes, 5 receipt or order scans a month | — |
| **Plus** | $2.99/mo or $24.99/yr | Unlimited scans and pasted orders, week planner (with an iftar/suhoor planner in Ramadan), nutrition, Ask Remi | You shop more than 5 times a month, or you want the planner |
| **Device bundle (later)** | not priced | Only after a bill of materials exists and the app shows week-4 retention | — |

Regional pricing: use the stores' own price tiers. Charge around EGP 79 a month in Egypt and around AED 10.99 / SAR 10.99 in the Gulf. **Check the store tier tables before setting these.**

## 5. The opening offer

**Founding Kitchen: $19.99 a year for the first 1,000 households, kept for as long as they stay. It closes at the end of Ramadan 2027** (around 9 March 2027, date to confirm) or at 1,000 households, whichever comes first. The cap is real, there's an end date, and no "was" price is invented. The cost is capped at 1,000 households, and it buys proof and reviews.

## 6. What to test with real buyers

1. **$2.99 vs $3.99 a month** on two store-listing variants (Google Play store-listing experiments) or two pre-sale pages. Measure trial → paid.
2. **Yearly-first vs monthly-first paywall**, at $24.99 vs $2.99. Measure paid conversion and the 60-day refund rate.
3. In Egypt only: **EGP 49 vs EGP 79** a month.
