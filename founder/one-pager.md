# Remi: one page

**Verdict: Not yet.** Strong product and cheap to run. Demand isn't proven.

**The problem.** Families throw out food they forgot they bought, and they buy the same things twice because the list lives in a WhatsApp group and in people's heads. The apps that track a kitchen don't speak Arabic, and the Arabic apps only have recipes.

**The customer.** Arabic-speaking families of 3 or more who cook at home. The first segment is Egyptian households in Cairo and in the Gulf, because 190 of the 400 recipes are Egyptian.

**The offer and price.**
- **Free:** kitchen, expiry reminders, a shared list for spouse and helper, all recipes, 5 scans a month.
- **Remi Plus:** $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year. Unlimited scans, a week planner (iftar/suhoor in Ramadan), nutrition, Ask Remi.
- **Founding Kitchen:** $19.99 a year for the first 1,000 households, until the end of Ramadan 2027.

**The numbers** (projections, founders unpaid)

| --- | --- |
| Contribution per paying household | $2.19 a month (73%) |
| Fixed costs | $108 a month |
| Break-even | 50 paying households |
| Year 1 operating result | +$782 |
| Cash needed | $924 |
| With one founder paid $1,000 a month | break-even 507 households; year 1 -$11,218 |

**The evidence**
- **Buyer panel (simulated):** 0 of 20 bought on the first offer and 1 of 20 on the revised offer, and that one only for the free tier. The main reason was the effort of logging (12 of 20).
- **Competitors:** no app combines Arabic, a pantry with expiry, and receipt logging. Kitchen app subscriptions run $2.99–6.99 a month.
- **Real test:** none yet.

**What the money is for.** About $924: developer accounts, a 20-receipt accuracy test, an expert review of allergen data, and a small creator test. No hardware spend.

**The ask.** 30 households for a 4-week pilot starting 14 December 2026. The pass mark is at least 40% still logging in week 4 and at least 8 of 30 paying the founding price.
