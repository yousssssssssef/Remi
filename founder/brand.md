# Brand: Remi

**Keep the name Remi and the "Sip Happy" look. Both are already built, distinctive, and nothing like the competitors' spreadsheet-style apps.** What the brand still needs is a promise that matches the new positioning, not the feature list, and the legal checks on the name before money goes into it.

## 1. The name

Don't rename. Remi is short, easy to say in Arabic (ريمي), works as a 32 px icon with the mascot's face, and the mascot gives the brand a character. The competitor map has no app named Remi (`competitors.md`).

The alternatives, only in case a trademark check blocks Remi:

| candidate | style | says | risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Remi | invented, character | a friendly kitchen helper | common first name; check the class 9/42 registers |
| Remi Kitchen / ريمي كيتشن | descriptive extension | Remi, clearly about food | longer; fallback if "Remi" alone is taken |
| Matbakhi (مطبخي, "my kitchen") | descriptive, Arabic | exactly the job | generic, hard to protect |
| Khazna (خزنة, "the store cupboard") | evocative | the pantry | sounds financial |

**Checks the founders must run:**

- **Trademark.** Search **Remi** and **ريمي** in Nice **class 9** (downloadable software) and **class 42** (SaaS):
  - Egypt: ITDA, the Egyptian trademark office
  - UAE: Ministry of Economy trademark services
  - Saudi Arabia: SAIP
  - WIPO Global Brand Database, for international registrations
  - USPTO and EUIPO, if you will sell outside MENA

  A trademark lawyer confirms the result. Nothing is protected until you file.
- **App store name.** Search both stores for "Remi". A store listing like "Remi: Kitchen & Groceries" avoids a clash with existing "Remi" apps. I haven't checked this; do it before submitting.
- **Domain.** Check the availability of remi.app, getremi.app, remikitchen.com and .com.eg / .ae. Don't assume any of them is free.
- **Handles.** Check Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube for @remi.kitchen, @remikitchen and @remi.app (Arabic handle: ريمي).

## 2. Promise, taglines, voice

- **The promise:** *Nothing you bought goes to waste without you knowing.*
- **Tagline options:**
  1. "Snap the receipt. Remi remembers." / "صوّر الفاتورة، وريمي يفتكر"
  2. "Cook what you have." / "اطبخ اللي عندك"
  3. "Your kitchen, shared." / "مطبخك، للبيت كله"
  - The current boot line, "Cooking made fun", is good for the device and the mascot. It's weak as a promise.
- **Voice:**

| adjective | do | don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm | talk like a family member who cooks ("the tomatoes need using today") | cute baby talk, too many emoji |
| Plain | one idea a line, in Egyptian or Gulf-friendly Modern Standard Arabic | marketing words ("revolutionary", "AI-powered") |
| Honest | "this is an estimate", "we couldn't read this line" | promises about health, weight loss or money saved that you can't prove |

**A line from the pitch, rewritten:**
- *Before:* "Snap your grocery receipt and Remi puts the food into your digital kitchen, shows what expires first, and suggests recipes (400, with photos) from what you already have."
- *After:* "Snap the receipt. Remi tells you what to use first and what to cook tonight."

## 3. The look (already in `DESIGN.md`; keep it)

| colour | hex | job | text contrast (WCAG AA) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lemon | #F8E45C | app background, brand recognition | dark navy or black text passes; **white text fails**, never use it |
| Cobalt | #1734BE | actions, buttons | white text passes (about 9:1) |
| Hot pink | #ED2B8C | the occasional pop: badges, the "use first" flag | white text is about 4:1, which passes only for large text (18 pt+ or 14 pt bold); use it for icons and large labels |
| Device gold | #F2C230 | the TFT screen only (lemon washes out on it) | — |

Run the exact contrast ratios through a checker before shipping; the figures above are approximate.

- **Type:** DM Sans (Google Fonts, open licence) for Latin headings, plus an Arabic face with a matching weight, such as IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Noto Sans Arabic (both open licence). Check that the Arabic face is the one actually shipping.
- **Logo:** the mascot's face as the app icon. It has to read at 32 px, on the lemon background and on dark mode. Avoid fridge and chef-hat clichés; the competitors use them.

## 4. The first five touchpoints

1. **Store listing (Arabic first):** a screenshot of the receipt turning into the "use first" list, and nothing else on the first screen.
2. **First run:** one receipt or a pasted order, then the "use first" list. The mascot waves once, and that's all.
3. **Paywall:** say what Free already does before you ask for money. Show the founding price with its real end date and cap.
4. **First social post:** the forgotten-coriander reel (hook 1).
5. **Reply to the first complaint:** in the same language, within 24 hours. Say what happened and what you'll fix. No canned text.
