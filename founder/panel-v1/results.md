## What would flip a no

- If it linked straight to the cooking videos I already watch and my helper could use it easily in Arabic without any setup.
- If my husband and I shared one list that updated automatically when either of us scanned a receipt, and it worked on the first try with Arabic receipts without any manual fixing, I'd pay for a year.
- Seeing that the recipes are mostly Gulf and Arab home cooking, with a Ramadan mode for iftar and suhoor planning, and that the free version already works well for my household.
- If the free version were enough to catch what's about to expire, or if Plus were a one-time payment of a few dollars with no recurring charge.
- If it let me save a recipe straight from an Instagram or TikTok video and showed me what I'm missing from what I already have, and the paid part stayed free for a good trial.
- If it counted calories and protein for the meals my helper cooks, with the dishes in Arabic and local food, and my helper could use it easily in Arabic without me doing the scanning.
- Clear proof that my data stays private and isn't sold, plus receipt scanning that works with my Talabat or Noon orders automatically, so I don't have to do anything.
- If the receipt scan truly did everything on its own, including Arabic receipts from Carrefour, and a free month showed it saved me real food waste without me touching anything else.
- A clear promise in Arabic that my receipts and household data stay private, plus the free version proving useful for a few weeks before I ever pay.
- If nutrition and calorie tracking worked well on its own with Arabic dishes, was free or cheap, and the household could add the groceries without scanning receipts.
- Seeing that the recipes are mostly Gulf and Arabic dishes, and that my wife and the helper can both use the shared list in Arabic as easily as WhatsApp.
- Seeing it read a real Arabic receipt or a Talabat/Noon order correctly, or a free trial of Plus where I can test it on my own groceries.
